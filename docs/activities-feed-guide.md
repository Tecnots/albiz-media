# Activities Feed — How It Works

## What is the algorithm?

The Activities feed does not show posts in the order they were posted. Instead it ranks every post and shows the most relevant ones first — for you specifically, not for everyone.

The algorithm is the same system used by X (Twitter) for their "For You" feed.

---

## The 6 Tabs

### For You
Your personal feed. Shows posts from people you follow mixed with posts from people you don't follow yet. The posts most likely to interest you appear first.

### Following
Only shows posts from people you follow. The people you interact with most (like, comment, read) appear higher than people you follow but never engage with.

### Trending
Shows what is gaining attention right now across the whole platform. A post that got 50 likes in the last 2 hours ranks higher than a post with 500 likes from last week.

### News
Only posts tagged as News, ranked by how fast they are gaining attention.

### AI
Only posts tagged as AI, Machine Learning, or Deep Learning — ranked the same way.

### Technology
Only posts tagged as Technology, Tech, Software, or Hardware — ranked the same way.

---

## How the Feed Decides What to Show First

The algorithm looks at 3 things:

**1. Your interests**
When you select topics in preferences (Business, AI, Technology etc.), posts matching those topics always appear before posts that don't match. Interest posts get a 3× ranking boost.

**2. Who you follow**
Posts from people you follow get ranked significantly higher than posts from strangers. Following someone is a signal that you want to see their content.

**3. How engaging the post is**
A comment means more than a like. A like means more than just a view. The algorithm estimates how likely you are to engage with each post based on your history.

---

## How It Learns From You

Every action you take teaches the feed what to show next.

| What you do | What the feed learns |
|---|---|
| Like a post | Show more from this author and topic |
| Comment on a post | Strong signal — show much more like this |
| Read a post for 5+ seconds | You actually read it — keep showing similar content |
| Scroll past quickly | You didn't want to see it |
| Click "Not interested" | Never show this kind of post again |
| Mute someone | Remove all their posts from your feed permanently |
| Follow someone from a post | Strongest signal — prioritise this author |

---

## Why You Might See Someone You Don't Follow

The feed always mixes content from people you follow with content from people you don't follow yet. This is so you can discover new voices. Out-of-network posts appear because they match your interests or because people you follow have engaged with them.

---

## Why the Feed Changes Every Time

The feed is never the same twice. Each time you open it, posts are re-ranked based on your most recent activity. As you engage more, the feed becomes more accurate.

New users see content based on the topics they selected. After a few weeks of activity, the feed becomes personalised to exactly what you read and engage with.

---

## What the Feed Never Shows You

- Posts you've already seen (they sink to the bottom, not completely hidden)
- Posts from people you've muted or blocked
- Your own posts
- Posts marked "Not interested"
